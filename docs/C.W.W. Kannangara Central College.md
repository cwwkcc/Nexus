## Who We Are

C.W.W. Kannangara Central College is not just a school. It is the school that changed Sri Lanka.

Founded in 1873 in Mathugama, it became the nation's first Central College in 1941 — the living proof of Dr. C.W.W. Kannangara's belief that a child born in a village deserves the same education as a child born in Colombo. The Free Education Act that followed in 1944 transformed the entire nation. This school was the beginning of that transformation.

We are the oldest Central College in Sri Lanka. We are the most populated school in the Kalutara District. We are the institution that broke the two-tier system. Our students are called **Kannangarians** — and that name carries weight.

## The Name

**C.W.W. Kannangara Central College, Mathugama**

Named after Dr. Christopher William Wijekoon Kannangara (1884–1969), Minister of Education and Father of Free Education in Sri Lanka. He chose Mathugama — his own electorate — as the site of the first Central College, ensuring the experiment had direct ministerial commitment.

The name is never shortened in formal contexts. In informal and community use, the school is known as:

- **KCC** — the most common abbreviation
- **Kannangara** — used by alumni and the wider community
- **Mathugama Central** — used in regional sporting and civic contexts
- **Kannangarians** — what alumni and students call themselves

## Core Identity

### The Motto

> **"Wisdom is All Wealth"** _සු​ඛෝ පඤ්ඤාය පඨිලාභෝ — Panna Naranam Ratanam_

This is not a slogan. It is a declaration of values. In a country where rural families had been denied access to quality education for generations, this motto asserted that knowledge — not land, not money, not status — is the truest form of wealth. Every student who passes through these gates inherits that assertion.

### The Vision

> _"To gift the world great humans who are endowed with virtues and wisdom."_

### The Mission

> _"To reward the nation with noble sons and daughters possessing balanced personalities, dedicated to serving their country and religion — planning and implementing teaching and learning to develop head, heart, and hand."_

### The Three-Fold Philosophy — Head, Heart, Hand

Dr. Kannangara's educational philosophy was built on three pillars:

- **Head** — Cognitive development. Academic rigor. The pursuit of knowledge.
- **Heart** — Moral character. Ethical maturity. Empathy and service.
- **Hand** — Practical skill. Technical ability. The capacity to build and create.

This triad is not a metaphor — it is the literal architecture of the curriculum and extracurricular programme. A student who excels academically but lacks character has only developed one of three pillars. The school demands all three.

### The Three Core Values

**Truth · Courage · Discipline**

These values have been instilled in students since 1873. They are taught not as rules but as character — through scouting, cadets, sports, and the daily ritual of paying homage to Dr. Kannangara's statue at the school entrance.

## The Founder

### Dr. C.W.W. Kannangara (1884–1969)

Born in Randombe, Hikkaduwa. Son of a rural family. Beneficiary of a free Wesleyan missionary school. Scholarship student at Richmond College, Galle — where he led the British Empire in Mathematics in 1903. Lawyer. Politician. Minister of Education. Father of Free Education.

His personal journey from rural poverty to national leadership is the story this school tells every day. Students pay homage to his statue upon entering school — not as ritual, but as a reminder: _someone fought for your right to be here._

**Key dates:**

- 1903 — Led British Empire in Mathematics at Cambridge Senior examinations
- 1931 — First Minister of Education, State Council of Ceylon
- 1936–1947 — Represented Matugama constituency
- 1941 — Established the first Central College at Mathugama
- 1944 — Free Education Bill passed (May 30 – June 2, 1944)
- 1969 — Passed away, leaving a system that educates millions
- 2011 — Alumni commission statue erected at school entrance

**The opposition he faced:** Prime Minister D.S. Senanayake questioned whether education would make it difficult to find boys for coconut plucking. Kannangara's response was the Free Education Act — one of the longest speeches in the history of the legislature, delivered to thunderous applause.

## Visual Identity

### School Colors

|Color|Usage|
|---|---|
|**Forest Green**|Primary institutional color. Used in uniforms, sports, flags, and the school's primary brand. The "Men in Green" identity in sports is built on this.|
|**Gold**|Accent color. Represents the lamp of knowledge in the crest, the warmth of the motto, the earned achievement of the institution.|
|**Warm Cream**|Background and surface color. Reflects the physical character of the school — cream walls, warm light, aged institutional stone.|

The **Nexus digital color system** translates these into:

- Forest Green → `#1A4A2E`
- Gold → `#C9973A` / `#E8B84B`
- Warm Cream → `#F7F3EC` / `#EDE8DF`

### The Crest

The school crest is the center of all visual identity. Every design decision in Nexus radiates outward from the crest. Its symbols:

|Symbol|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**The Lamp (Pahana)**|The light of education driving away ignorance. The reason gold appears throughout the design.|
|**The Lotus**|Purity and potential — rising from murky water to bloom perfectly, regardless of circumstances.|
|**The Dharmachakra**|Truth, righteousness, the endless pursuit of wisdom.|
|**The Laurel**|Achievement, honour, recognition of excellence.|

The crest is never used decoratively. It is used purposefully — at the center of the homepage hero, on the loading screen, and as the anchor of the school's digital identity.

### The Flag

The school flag is a formal symbol of institutional pride, used at major ceremonies and sporting events.

### Logo Assets

The college maintains professional SVG and PNG logo assets for consistent institutional use across digital and print.

## The Kannangarian Identity

Students and alumni of KCC are called **Kannangarians**. This is not a nickname — it is a badge of belonging that connects generations of graduates across professions, decades, and borders.

Kannangarians are found in medicine, engineering, law, business, and government — both locally and internationally. The Old Boys' Association (OBA) is an active alumni network that has contributed to the school's development, including commissioning the founder's statue in 2011.

The Kannangarian identity is defined by:

- The motto — _Wisdom is All Wealth_
- The values — Truth, Courage, Discipline
- The philosophy — Head, Heart, Hand
- The colours — The green of the uniform, the gold of achievement
- The heritage — Being part of the institution that started it all

## Academic Identity

- **5,000+ students** — largest school in the Kalutara District
- **200+ staff** — dedicated academic professionals

- **National School** status — premier tier of Sri Lankan education

## Extracurricular Identity

### "Men in Green"

The school's sporting identity. The green uniform worn by KCC athletes in cricket, athletics, and other sports carries the weight of the institution's competitive tradition. The annual **Pasdun Cricket Battle** against Sri Palee Vidyalaya, Horana (inaugurated 2017) is the school's premier sporting event.

### Scout Association

Founded 1952 by Hilary Silva. Over 35 President's Scout Award winners. First recipient: Neyomal Kannangara. First Wood Badge recipient: Namal Priyantha Gurusinghe. One of the most decorated Scout troops in Sri Lanka.

### National Cadet Corps

Regular participants in annual battalion camps at the Rantambe National Cadet Training Centre.

### Music

Five distinct musical bands:

- Two Western junior bands
- One senior girls' Western band
- One boys' Western brass band (newly formed)
- Participation in Poson Bathi Gee recitals and civic ceremonies

### Kannangara ICT Society (KITS)

The school's technology society. Responsible for building and maintaining the school's digital presence, including the Nexus website.

## Community Identity

KCC is not just a school for its students. It is a civic institution for Mathugama and the Kalutara District.

- The school ground (4 acres, helipad-equipped) serves as a regional gathering space
- The auditorium is a primary venue for community events and corporate gatherings
- The swimming pool serves the broader community including external schools and organisations
- The School Development Society (SDS) links the school to the community
- The Old Boys' Association (OBA) connects generations of alumni

The school was established by the community, for the community — and it continues to serve that purpose 153 years later.

## What Makes KCC Different

Every school claims to be excellent. KCC's difference is specific and provable:

1. **We were first.** The first Central College in Sri Lanka. Not one of the first. The first.
2. **We carry the name.** Named after the man who changed the educational destiny of the entire nation.
3. **We started a movement.** The Free Education system that educated millions of Sri Lankans began here.
4. **We serve everyone.** Not just our students — our community, our district, our nation.
5. **We are still evolving.** From vernacular school in 1873 to digital institution in 2026. KITS built Nexus. Students built Paideon. The school that started it all is still leading.

## Brand Voice

### Tone

- **Authoritative but warm** — 153 years earns authority; the mission of free education demands warmth
- **Proud but not arrogant** — pride in heritage, humility in service
- **Timeless but not old** — the motto is ancient; KITS is building the future
- **Specific not vague** — "first Central College" not "leading educational institution"

### What we say

- We talk about the mission — _education as a right, not a privilege_
- We talk about the people — Dr. Kannangara, the Kannangarians, the community
- We talk about the proof — exam results, scout awards, KITS achievements
- We talk about the future 

### What we don't say

- Generic educational platitudes — "nurturing young minds," "shaping tomorrow's leaders"
- Anything that could apply to any other school
- Anything that doesn't trace back to Dr. Kannangara's original vision

## Relationship to KITS and Nexus

**KITS** (Kannangara ICT Society) is the student body building the school's digital future. Nexus is their flagship project — the official school website, built entirely by students.

This relationship matters for the brand: **Nexus is itself proof of the Head, Heart, Hand philosophy.** Students applying their technical skills (Hand), their intellectual rigour (Head), and their commitment to the institution (Heart) to serve the community.

The Nexus website should feel like an extension of the institution it represents — not a student project, but a product worthy of a 153-year-old National School.
