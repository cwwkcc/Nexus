# Nexus — Editorial Style Guide

**Tone, voice, and style for all content**

---

## Purpose

This document defines the editorial voice and style for all content on the Nexus platform.

---

## Voice and Tone

### Core Voice

**The school speaks as one voice.**

- **Confident** — Not arrogant
- **Knowledgeable** — Not show-off
- **Warm** — Not over-familiar
- **Professional** — Not stiff
- **Welcoming** — Not exclusive
- **Proud** — Not boastful

### Tone Variations

| Content Type | Tone |
|--------------|------|
| News articles | Informative, factual, professional |
| Events | Exciting, inclusive, action-oriented |
| About pages | Reflective, proud, historical |
| Academic content | Clear, educational, inspiring |
| Admissions | Encouraging, clear, helpful |
| Social media | Engaging, concise, warm |

### Voice Qualities

**1. Educational**

We inform and guide. We don't just tell — we teach.

**Good:** "Our students develop skills in critical thinking, problem-solving, and collaboration through our integrated curriculum."

**Bad:** "Our students learn the most important skills."

**2. Heritage-focused**

We are proud of our history and traditions.

**Good:** "Since 1873, we have shaped the minds that shape Sri Lanka."

**Bad:** "We are a really old school."

**3. Respectful**

We treat all members of our community with respect.

**Good:** "We welcome students from all backgrounds and traditions."

**Bad:** "We accept anyone who applies."

**4. Future-oriented**

We honour the past while building the future.

**Good:** "Building on our 153-year legacy, we prepare students for the challenges of tomorrow."

**Bad:** "We are moving forward."

## Style Rules

### Capitalisation

- **Proper nouns:** Capitalise school name, society names, event names
- **Titles:** Capitalise Principal, Vice Principal, Head of Department
- **Streams:** Capitalise Science, Commerce, Arts, Technology
- **Exams:** Capitalise O/L, A/L

### Punctuation

- **Full stops:** End all complete sentences with a full stop
- **Commas:** Use for lists and clarity
- **Apostrophes:** Use for possessives (not for plurals)
- **Quotes:** Use double quotes for direct speech

### Numbers

- **1-9:** Spell out (one, two, three)
- **10+:** Use numerals (10, 25, 100)
- **Dates:** 30 June 2026 (not 30/06/2026)
- **Years:** 1873 (not '73)

### Dates and Times

- **Date format:** 30 June 2026
- **Time format:** 2:30 PM (with space)
- **Duration:** 2 hours, 30 minutes

### Abbreviations

- **First use:** Spell out the full term
- **Second use:** Use the abbreviation
- **School:** C.W.W. Kannangara Central College (first use), KCC (subsequent)
- **Society:** Kannangara ICT Society (first use), KITS (subsequent)

### Headings

- **h1:** Page title (use once)
- **h2:** Main sections
- **h3:** Subsections
- **h4:** Sub-subsections (use sparingly)
- **Use title case** for h1 and h2
- **Use sentence case** for h3 and h4

### Lists

- **Bullet points:** For unordered lists
- **Numbered lists:** For ordered steps
- **Consistent punctuation:** Use full stops for complete sentences

## Common Mistakes

### Avoid

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| "We are the best school" | "We are a leading educational institution" |
| "Cutting-edge technology" | "Modern technology" |
| "World-class education" | "High-quality education" |
| "Click here" | Use descriptive link text |
| "Exciting news!" | "We are pleased to announce" |
| "Our amazing students" | "Our students" |

### Use

| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| Kids | Students, young people |
| Top-notch | High-quality, excellent |
| Awesome | Impressive, notable |
| Super | Very, extremely |
| Got | Received, achieved |

## School-Specific Terms

### Correct Terms

| Term | Usage |
|------|-------|
| C.W.W. Kannangara Central College | Full name (first use) |
| KCC | Abbreviation (subsequent use) |
| Mathugama | Location (spelled correctly) |
| Wisdom is All Wealth | Motto (with capital letters) |
| Est. 1873 | Established 1873 |
| Kannangarians | Alumni and students |
| Head, Heart, Hand | Philosophy (with capital letters) |
| Truth, Courage, Discipline | Values (with capital letters) |

### Avoid

| Term | Why Avoid |
|------|-----------|
| K Central | Not the official abbreviation |
| Mathugama Central | Informal, not official |
| Wisdom is Everything | Not the motto |
| Head Heart Hand | Missing Oxford comma |

## Example Content

### Good News Article

**Headline:** KITS Wins Gold at SLIIT Codefest 2026

**Opening:**
> We are proud to announce that the Kannangara ICT Society (KITS) has won the Gold Award at SLIIT Codefest 2026.

**Body:**
> This prestigious competition brought together the best young technology students from across Sri Lanka. Our team of five students demonstrated exceptional skill in software development, presenting a project that addressed real-world challenges in education technology.
>
> The team consisted of [names], under the guidance of [Teacher Name]. They worked for three months preparing their submission, which impressed the judges with its innovation and technical excellence.

**Closing:**
> This achievement reflects the dedication and talent of our students, who continue to excel in the field of technology. We look forward to their future successes.

### Good Event Listing

**Title:** School Sports Meet 2026

**Description:**
> Join us for the annual School Sports Meet at the KCC Sports Ground. Students from all grades will compete in athletics, team sports, and track events. The event will run from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, with lunch and refreshments available.

**Details:**
> - **Date:** 15 July 2026
> - **Time:** 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM
> - **Venue:** KCC Sports Ground
> - **Registration:** Students must register with their class teacher by 1 July 2026

- [ ] All information is accurate
- [ ] Spelling and grammar are correct
- [ ] Headings use correct hierarchy
- [ ] Dates and numbers are formatted correctly
- [ ] Links are descriptive
- [ ] Images have alt text
