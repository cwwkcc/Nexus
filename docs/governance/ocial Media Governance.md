# Nexus — Social Media Governance

**Policies for official school social media accounts**

---

## Purpose

Social media is a core component of Nexus — it is the school's digital face alongside the website. This document defines the governance framework for all official school social media accounts.

## Content Policy

### Content Pillars

| Pillar           | Description                        | Example                       |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Achievements** | Student and school accomplishments | Exam results, sports wins     |
| **Community**    | School life, events, activities    | Sports meets, cultural events |
| **Heritage**     | School history, traditions, values | Founder's legacy, anthem      |
| **Information**  | Important announcements            | Exam dates, admissions        |
| **Engagement**   | Interactive content                | Polls, Q&A, alumni spotlights |

### Content Mix

| Platform         | Content Mix                                      | Frequency        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| Facebook         | 60% Community, 20% Information, 20% Achievements | 3-5 posts/week   |
| Instagram        | 70% Community, 20% Achievements, 10% Engagement  | 3-5 posts/week   |
| YouTube          | 50% Events, 30% Heritage, 20% Achievements       | 1-2 videos/month |
| LinkedIn         | 40% Achievements, 30% Alumni, 30% News           | 1-2 posts/week   |
| WhatsApp Channel | 80% Information, 20% Urgent                      | As needed        |

### Prohibited Content

- Negative or defamatory content
- Inappropriate or offensive content
- Unverified information
- Personal information (without consent)
- Copyrighted content (without permission)

## Posting Guidelines

### Post Structure

**Facebook/Instagram:**

- 1-2 sentence introduction
- 2-3 paragraphs of content
- 1-3 high-quality images
- Relevant hashtags
- Call to action

**YouTube:**

- Title: Event name + description
- 1-2 paragraph description
- Relevant tags
- End screen with school logo and website

**WhatsApp Channel:**

- Short, direct message
- Clear call to action
- Link to website for details

### Frequency

| Platform         | Minimum       | Recommended    |
| ---------------- | ------------- | -------------- |
| Facebook         | 3 posts/week  | 5 posts/week   |
| Instagram        | 3 posts/week  | 5 posts/week   |
| YouTube          | 1 video/month | 2 videos/month |
| LinkedIn         | 1 post/week   | 2 posts/week   |
| WhatsApp Channel | 1 post/week   | As needed      |

### Best Times to Post

| Platform  | Best Times                          |
| --------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Facebook  | Weekdays 9 AM – 12 PM, 6 PM – 9 PM  |
| Instagram | Weekdays 11 AM – 2 PM, 6 PM – 9 PM  |
| YouTube   | Weekends 10 AM – 12 PM              |
| LinkedIn  | Weekdays 8 AM – 10 AM, 12 PM – 2 PM |

## Approval Workflow

### Standard Posts

1. **Draft** — Social Media Editor creates draft
2. **Review** — Staff Advisor reviews
3. **Approve** — Staff Advisor approves
4. **Schedule** — Editor schedules the post
5. **Publish** — Editor publishes

### Urgent Posts

1. **Draft** — Social Media Editor creates draft
2. **Phone Approval** — Staff Advisor approves by phone
3. **Publish** — Editor publishes

### Crisis Posts

1. **Draft** — Staff Advisor creates draft
2. **Review** — Principal reviews
3. **Approve** — Principal approves
4. **Publish** — Staff Advisor publishes
5. **Monitor** — Staff Advisor monitors responses

## Engagement Guidelines

### Responding to Comments

| Response Type     | Action                               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Positive comments | Like and respond with gratitude      |
| Negative comments | Respond professionally, take offline |
| Questions         | Answer promptly and accurately       |
| Spam              | Delete and block                     |

### Response Times

| Platform         | Response Time   |
| ---------------- | --------------- |
| Facebook         | Within 24 hours |
| Instagram        | Within 24 hours |
| YouTube          | Within 48 hours |
| WhatsApp Channel | Broadcast only  |

### Crisis Communication

1. **Pause** all scheduled posts
2. **Coordinate** with school administration
3. **Draft** a response
4. **Approval** by Principal
5. **Publish** single, unified response
6. **Monitor** all platforms

## Data Privacy

### Student Privacy

- No photos without consent
- No full names of students (use first name only or initials)
- No personal contact information
- No grades or results without permission

### Staff Privacy

- No photos without consent
- No personal contact information
- No performance information

### Publishing Requirements

- Photo consent form for all identifiable students
- Staff consent for photos and names
- Appropriate permissions for all content
