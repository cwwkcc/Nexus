# Nexus — Content Guidelines

**Writing, image, and accessibility standards**

---

## Purpose

This document defines the standards for all content published on the Nexus platform. All editors must follow these guidelines.

---

## Writing Guidelines

### Tone and Voice

**Tone:** Formal but welcoming — like a respected headmaster

- **Formal:** Professional, respectful, authoritative
- **Welcoming:** Warm, inclusive, encouraging
- **Educational:** Informative, clear, helpful
- **Heritage-focused:** Proud of history, respectful of traditions

**Voice:** The school speaks as one voice

- Consistent across all content
- Represents the school's values
- Respectful to all readers

**Do:**

- Use clear, simple language
- Be informative and helpful
- Show pride in the school's heritage
- Be respectful to all members of the community

**Don't:**

- Use marketing jargon ("world-class", "cutting-edge")
- Use excessive exclamation marks
- Use corporate language ("leverage", "synergy")
- Be overly emotional or pleading
- Use slang or informal language

---

### Writing Style

**Structure:**

- Use headings to organise content
- Keep paragraphs short (3-5 sentences)
- Use bullet points for lists
- Include a clear call to action where appropriate

**Grammar:**

- Use active voice where possible
- Maintain consistent verb tense
- Use Oxford comma (optional, but consistent)
- Check spelling and grammar

**Length:**

- News articles: 300-500 words
- Page content: 200-400 words per section
- Event descriptions: 100-200 words
- Society descriptions: 100-200 words

---

### Examples

**Good:**

> "We are proud to announce that the Kannangara ICT Society has won the Gold Award at the SLIIT Codefest 2026. This achievement reflects the dedication and skill of our students, who continue to excel in the field of technology."

**Bad:**

> "We are super excited to announce that KITS has rocked it again at SLIIT Codefest! Our amazing students are the best in the country, and they keep making us proud! 🎉"

## Image Guidelines

### Quality Standards

- **Resolution:** High-resolution (minimum 1200px width)
- **Format:** WebP preferred (JPEG/PNG fallback)
- **Size:** Optimised for web (max 2MB)
- **Content:** In-focus, well-lit, appropriate

### Content Standards

- **Real students:** Use actual school photos, not stock photography
- **Campus:** Showcase school buildings, grounds, facilities
- **Events:** Capture authentic moments, not staged photos
- **People:** Use candid shots where possible

### Alt Text Standards

All images must have alt text:

- **Descriptive:** Describe what is in the image
- **Concise:** Keep under 125 characters
- **Contextual:** Provide the information the image conveys
- **No "image of":** Start with the description

**Good alt text:**

> "Students participating in the school sports meet at the athletics track"

**Bad alt text:**

> "image of students at sports meet" (too vague)
> "Sports meet 2026" (doesn't describe the image)

## Accessibility Standards

### General

All content must meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA:

- **Colour contrast:** Normal text ≥4.5:1
- **Headings:** Use proper heading hierarchy (h1, h2, h3)
- **Links:** Use descriptive link text (not "click here")
- **Images:** All images must have alt text
- **Forms:** All forms must have labels

### Headings

- Use one h1 per page
- Use h2 for main sections
- Use h3 for subsections
- Do not skip heading levels

### Links

- Use descriptive link text
- Do not use "click here" or "read more" (use specific text)
- Open external links in a new tab (with appropriate warning)
- Use relative URLs for internal links

**Good link text:**

> "Learn more about our [academic streams](/academics)"
> "Read the full article on [our achievements](/news/achievements-2026)"

**Bad link text:**

> "Click here to learn more"
> "Read more"

## SEO Guidelines

### Titles

- Unique title for every page
- 50-60 characters maximum
- Include relevant keywords
- Start with the most important information

### Descriptions

- Unique description for every page
- 150-160 characters maximum
- Include relevant keywords
- Compelling and descriptive

### Headings

- Use h1 for page title
- Use h2 for main sections
- Include relevant keywords in headings
- Create a clear hierarchy

### Image Optimisation

- Use descriptive filenames
- Use descriptive alt text
- Compress images for web
- Use WebP format where possible

## Social Media Guidelines

### Post Structure

- **Facebook/Instagram:** 1-2 sentence intro, 2-3 paragraphs, 1-3 images
- **YouTube:** Title + description, tags, end screen
- **WhatsApp Channel:** Short, direct message with clear CTA
- **LinkedIn:** Professional tone, 2-3 paragraphs

### Brand Guidelines

- **Profile photo:** School crest
- **Cover photo:** School building or event photo
- **Colours:** Use school colours where possible
- **Tagging:** Tag relevant accounts (OBA, other societies)

### Hashtags

- `#KCC`
- `#Kannangarians`
- `#WisdomIsAllWealth`
- `#CWWKCC`
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
