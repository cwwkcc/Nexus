# Nexus — Governance Overview

**Policies and procedures for content management**

---

## Purpose

This governance framework ensures that Nexus remains a living, accurate, and professional digital institution. It defines:

- **Who** is responsible for what content
- **How** content is created, reviewed, and published
- **When** content should be updated or archived
- **What** standards content must meet

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Ownership

Every piece of content has a named owner responsible for its accuracy and timeliness.

### 2. Quality

All content meets established standards for writing, accessibility, and visual identity.

### 3. Timeliness

Content is reviewed regularly and updated promptly when information changes.

### 4. Accountability

All changes are tracked in the audit log. Every editor is accountable for their actions.

### 5. Accessibility

Content is accessible to all users, regardless of ability or device.
